## Brand & Style

This design system embodies curated minimalism with razor-sharp editorial execution. It caters to senior professionals, designers, developers, and creative leaders who want to project authority, meticulous craft, and modern technological fluency without unnecessary ornamental noise.

The aesthetic fuses crisp modernist Swiss typography with an assertive electric blue pulse. The emotional tone is decisive, refined, and confident. Generous negative space frames work artifacts, treating case studies and projects with the dignity of physical gallery installations while retaining the kinetic snap of high-performance digital interfaces.

## Colors

The palette establishes an uncompromisingly luminous base where electric royal blue (`#0D52FE`) commands attention against sterile, balanced whites and deep architectural grays.

- **Primary (`#0D52FE`)**: Pure kinetic royal blue. Reserved strictly for primary calls-to-action, active indicators, interactive hover targets, and vital milestones.
- **Secondary (`#1B2432`)**: Deep ink slate. Anchors high-priority structural frames, inverted surfaces, badges, and primary iconography.
- **Tertiary (`#50637F`)**: Neutral cool steel. Governs secondary supporting information, timestamps, metadata labels, and structural hairpins.
- **Neutral (`#0B0D12`)**: Deep obsidian black. Delivers optimum contrast for core headings, reading copy, and fundamental boundaries.

Backgrounds utilize stark white (`#FFFFFF`) layered across subtle cool-tinted canvas backdrops (`#F8FAFC`). Borders maintain a delicate, surgical hairline using low-saturation translucent slate (`rgba(11, 13, 18, 0.08)`).

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances structural precision with technical flair:

- **Display & Headlines (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers geometric presence, warmth, and high-impact readability. Letter spacing tightens moderately as size scales upward to maintain dense editorial visual rhythm.
- **Body & Longform (`Inter`)**: Deployed for frictionless readability across project breakdowns, articles, and biographical descriptions. Line heights are spaced generously to ensure long-form eye comfort.
- **Labels, Metrics & Technical Tags (`Space Grotesk`)**: Provides a technical, engineered sub-voice. Employed on metadata tags, index numbering, role markers, and dates to ground creative portfolio assets.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a 12-column responsive fluid grid pinned to an absolute maximum content containment of `1360px`.

- **Breakpoints**: Mobile (`< 768px`, 4 columns), Tablet (`768px - 1024px`, 8 columns), Desktop (`> 1024px`, 12 columns).
- **Margins & Gutters**: Outer canvas margins expand from `1.25rem` on mobile devices up to `4rem` on desktop displays. Column gutters scale from `1.5rem` to `2rem`.
- **Rhythm**: Spacing follows a vertical rhythm rooted in standard 8px multiples. Sectional transitions rely on `space-2xl` and `space-3xl` gaps to maintain visual clarity and gallery-like breathing room between project presentations.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established primarily through crisp perimeter hairline boundaries paired with subtle ambient diffusion. Rather than relying on heavy skeuomorphic drop shadows, the system feels clean, lightweight, and modern:

- **Surface Base**: Clean matte paper white (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Surface Raised (Cards & Panels)**: Elevated using an ultra-subtle ambient shadow: `0 8px 30px rgba(11, 13, 18, 0.04), 0 1px 3px rgba(11, 13, 18, 0.02)` coupled with a continuous `1px` border of `rgba(11, 13, 18, 0.06)`.
- **Floating Modals & Flyouts**: `0 20px 50px rgba(13, 82, 254, 0.08), 0 6px 16px rgba(11, 13, 18, 0.04)`.
- **Interactive State Elevation**: Upon hover, project cards do not dramatically leap upward. Instead, they shift by a micro `-2px` translateY while the border transitions to a refined blue wash (`rgba(13, 82, 254, 0.3)`) and triggers a subtle blue-tinted halo: `0 16px 40px rgba(13, 82, 254, 0.08)`.

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp architectural lines with friendly tactile curves (`roundedness: 2`):

- **Standard Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges)**: Feature a balanced `0.5rem` (`8px`) border radius.
- **Containers & Project Cards**: Use `1rem` (`16px`) border radius, establishing a polished frame for photographic media and video embeds.
- **Pills & Status Tags**: Use `9999px` full round pill shapes to create distinct contrast against rectangular framing elements.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid electric blue (`#0D52FE`) background with white typography (`#FFFFFF`). `0.5rem` radius. Subtle hover transitions to `#0A42CE` with an ambient glow shadow.
- **Secondary / Outline**: Stark white background, `1px` border (`rgba(11, 13, 18, 0.12)`), text `#0B0D12`. Hover triggers a slate tint (`#F8FAFC`) and darkens the border.
- **Ghost**: Zero background, typography in `#0D52FE` or `#1B2432`, underline animation originating from left-to-right on hover.

### Cards & Project Showcases
- **Project Feature Card**: Border radius of `1rem`. Wrapped in a `1px` border (`rgba(11, 13, 18, 0.07)`). Aspect ratio of internal media set to 16:10 or 4:3. Textual content uses high-contrast headlines paired with `label-md` category tags. Hover evokes a subtle `-2px` lift and an accent perimeter transition.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Availability Chip**: Full pill radius (`9999px`), `label-sm` font, featuring an active electric pulse dot (e.g., "Available for Q3 projects"). Background in translucent blue (`rgba(13, 82, 254, 0.08)`) with `#0D52FE` text.
- **Skill / Tech Stack Tag**: Subtle background `#F1F5F9`, `#1B2432` text, `0.375rem` radius, lightweight and compact padding (`0.25rem 0.625rem`).

### Form Elements & Inputs
- **Text Fields**: Clean flat backgrounds (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` subtle outline (`rgba(11, 13, 18, 0.15)`). Text styled with `body-md`. Focus state shifts the border immediately to `#0D52FE` with a `0 0 0 3px rgba(13, 82, 254, 0.15)` ring.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Micro `0.25rem` radius on checkboxes, complete circle for radio indicators. Checked state adopts `#0D52FE` with sharp white glyphs.

### Lists & Timelines (Experience / Awards)
- **Experience Row**: Clean horizontal layout separated by single-pixel divider rules (`rgba(11, 13, 18, 0.06)`). Left side displays company and role using `headline-sm`, right side pins year and duration utilizing `label-md` in `#50637F`.
